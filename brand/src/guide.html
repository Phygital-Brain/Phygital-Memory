Phygital
Memory

Locked notes your agent can remember.

The idea

The knot: one wire ties a loop inside the seal, like a string tied round a finger so you remember. Both ends are sparks: you and your agent. The loop is what's held safe between them.

The feeling

Warm, clear, made by a person. Same family as Phygital Brain and Phygital Key: the round seal, wires and sparks, flat surfaces, three fonts. Dark is the default theme, light is a full second theme.

Colour

Lime is you.
Ultramarine is the spark.

Lime is your memory and you: the logo, buttons, links, focus. Ultramarine is the spark: your agent at work, a note it opened, a door that needs a yes. Status always carries a word, never colour alone.

Bright lime #C8F031 is a logo and fill colour. As text on a light ground it nearly disappears (1.2:1), so light mode uses deep lime #566E00 (5.4:1). Ultramarine #4D5BFF is too dark for text on night (3.9:1), so dark mode uses #8A94FF (7.2:1) and light mode #3B47E6 (6.0:1). Night text on lime is 14.8:1, white text on ultramarine 4.9:1.

Rules
LogoNever recolour, redraw or rotate. Below 32px use the favicon file, which has a thicker wire and a simpler knot. Write the name exactly as "Phygital Memory".
ShapeCards 10px radius, buttons and fields 6px, tags and rows 4px, the seal always round.
DepthFlat with a hairline. No gradients, no glow.
WordsPlain and short. Say "your agent", not a product name. Tagline: "Locked notes your agent can remember."
