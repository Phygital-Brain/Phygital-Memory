# Code of conduct

Be kind and be clear. We want this project to be a friendly place for people who have never used a terminal and for people who live in one.

- Be respectful. No harassment, insults or discrimination.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you decide someone meant harm.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Never share other people's private information. That includes the contents of anyone's notes.

If someone breaks these rules, report it privately through the **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and say it is about conduct. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block people who break the rules.

This is a short version of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
