## Always say where it came from

Any project that uses Phygital Memory, changed or not, must say so in its README or about page, with a link to this repository. Use one of these: "built on Phygital Memory", "based on Phygital Memory" or "a fork of Phygital Memory".

This applies to every project.

## You can

- Use, change and share the code and text, under the license.
- Say true things about it, like the lines above.
- Show the logo next to a link to this repository, unchanged, when you talk about the project.

## You can't, without written permission

- Sell, offer or name an app, product, service or setup as "Phygital Memory", or with a name that could be mixed up with it.
- Use the logo, or something close to it, for your own app, product, service or account.
- Suggest that your version is the official one, or that Phygital-Brain made, checked or supports it.

A changed version you share needs its own name and its own app icon, so people can tell it apart from this app and its updates.

To ask for permission, open an issue on this repository.
