## Profile structure

1. Info block (see `Relationships/CLAUDE.md`), with `type: {client | collaborator | team | partner}`
2. Who they are: what they do, industry
3. Contacts: one line per person: `Name: email / phone (role, notes)`
4. How it started
5. Work together: every project or piece of work, with month and year
6. Notes: relationship quality, pricing notes, next opportunity

## Leads

`_Leads.md` (created on first use) holds leads that aren't clients yet, in two sections: `## New` and `## Keep warm`. One line each: date, name, what they want, where it came from. See `Relationships/CLAUDE.md`.
