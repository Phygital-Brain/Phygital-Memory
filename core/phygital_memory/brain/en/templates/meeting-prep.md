A short brief before a call or a meeting, from what this brain already knows.

Trigger: "prep me for {person or company}", "prep me for my next meeting", "brief me on {name}", or the same in {Language}.

## 1. Find who

- A name: search `Relationships/` for the person or company, including `aliases`. A contact person lives inside their company's note.
- "My next meeting": if a calendar is connected, read the next event and its attendees. If not, ask who the meeting is with.
- No note for them: say so in one line, then give what the daily notes and projects say about them, if anything.

## 2. Read

In this order, and only what you need:

1. Their note in `Relationships/`: who they are, the current state, the work together, notes.
2. Projects that link to them: "Status and next step".
3. The last 30 days of daily notes that mention them.
4. Open tasks in `Daily/tasks.md` and project pages that mention them.
5. Open decisions in `Memory/decisions/` that involve them.
6. Leads in `Relationships/Clients/_Leads.md`, if they're one.

## 3. Reply

Eight short lines at most:

- Who they are, in one line, and when you last talked (`last-contact`).
- What's going on now: the projects and their next steps.
- What was agreed last time.
- What {Name} owes them, and what they owe {Name}.
- One or two things to ask or bring up.

Every person, company and project as a `[[link]]`. Don't write a file. If {Name} wants it saved, put it in today's daily note.

## 4. After the meeting

If {Name} tells you how it went, save it the usual way (root rule 9): a line in today's note, the person's `last-contact`, the project's next step, and a decision note if something was decided.
