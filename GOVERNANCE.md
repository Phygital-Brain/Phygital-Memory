# Governance

How decisions are made in Phygital Memory. Short on purpose.

## Who decides

Phygital Memory is run by [Phygital Brain](https://github.com/Phygital-Brain), the maintainer. It is a small project run in spare time. The maintainer has the final say on what is merged, released and removed.

## How decisions are made

- Small changes (fixes, docs, translations): the maintainer reviews and merges. Anyone can propose one with a pull request.
- Bigger changes (a new AI tool, a change to a safety rule, a new dependency, a new place where data can go): open an issue first. The maintainer answers within a week and says yes, no or not now, with the reason.
- The safety rules in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) are not weakened without a test and a written reason.
- The repository is private for now. Until it opens, the maintainer decides who gets access.

## Becoming a maintainer

People who send good, careful changes over time can be invited to review and merge. The maintainer asks them in private and adds them to `.github/CODEOWNERS`.

## Disagreements

Say what you think in the issue or pull request, with a concrete example. If there is no agreement, the maintainer decides and writes down why.
