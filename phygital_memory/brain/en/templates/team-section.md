## Team

This brain is shared. Every person who works in it reads these rules.

| Person | Role | Their daily notes | Can change |
|---|---|---|---|
| {Name} | Owner | `Daily/` | Everything |
| {Person} | {role} | `Daily/{person}/` | {folders, for example Projects/, Relationships/Clients/} |

- **Who's talking:** at the start of a chat, if it isn't clear who you're working for, ask once: "Who am I working with?" Write session logs to that person's daily notes.
- **Shared facts, one place:** About, Projects, Relationships and Memory are shared. Don't keep a private copy of a shared fact.
- **Decisions say who decided.** Every decision note names the people who made it.
- **Each person's own rules:** a correction from one person goes under a `### {Person}'s rules` heading at the end of this section, unless they say it's for everyone (then it goes in `{Name}'s Rules` as usual).
- **Ask the owner** before changing rule files, About pages, or anything outside the folders a person can change.
- **Private folders:** what {Name} lists in `.claudeignore` stays closed for everyone. Folder permissions themselves are set in the notes app (for example Baalda), not here.
