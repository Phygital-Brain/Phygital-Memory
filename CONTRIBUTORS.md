# Contributors

Thank you to everyone who made Phygital Memory better. Code is one way to help. Bug reports, ideas, translations, docs, design and answers in issues count the same.

Add yourself in your pull request, or ask in an issue and the maintainer adds you. Ask first before you list someone else: some people want to stay private. Newest first.

| Who | What they did |
|---|---|
| [@Phygital-Brain](https://github.com/Phygital-Brain) | Created and maintains the project |

Kinds of contribution: code, bug reports, ideas, docs, translations (especially a native speaker reading a language), design, testing, reviews, answering questions, security reports, tutorials, talks.

Security researchers are credited in the published advisory and in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md), unless they prefer to stay anonymous.
