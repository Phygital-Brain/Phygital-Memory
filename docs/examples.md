# Things to ask

For when you have connected an AI app and are not sure what to say. You talk to your AI in plain words, in your own language. It picks the right tool.

> [!TIP]
> If your app does not use the tools by itself, start with *"Use the Phygital Memory tools:"*.

## Find and read

- *What did I decide about pricing last month?*
- *Search my notes for the call with the printer and tell me what was agreed.*
- *Open my project plan and give me the three next steps.*
- *Which notes link to my "Weekly review" note?*
- *What changed in my notes this week?*
- *Read the Word file in the Contracts folder and summarize it.*

## Write and add

These need **Allow edits** for that app.

- *Add a line to my project note: waiting on the client for the logo.*
- *Make a new note called Ideas/summer-trip.md with a packing list.*
- *Change "draft" to "final" in the Launch plan note.*
- *Put a short summary at the top of my meeting note from today.*

## Tidy up

- *Make a folder called Archive/2026 and move last year's meeting notes into it.*
- *Rename Plan.md to Plan 2027.md.*
- *Put the old drafts in the trash.*

## Give it a task

In the app, press **Give AI a task** on a note, or type a line under **To do** in the `AI tasks` note. Then ask:

- *Do my Phygital Memory tasks.*
- *Show me what is waiting in my AI tasks.*

Your AI reads each task with its note, does what it asks, and ticks it off with a short line of what it did.

## Undo

- *Undo the last change.* (Your AI cannot press Undo for you. Open **Activity** in the app and press **Undo** next to the change.)
- *Restore the note as it was before you changed it.* (Open the note's **Version history** and pick the version.)
- *Bring back the note I trashed.* (Open **Trash** and restore it. It stays for 7 days.)

## Keep it private

- *Look in my Private folder and tell me what is there.* The AI will ask first, because the folder is **Private for AI**. Say yes in the chat if you want it.
